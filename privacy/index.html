3. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser automatically sends technical data to GitHub's servers: your IP address, date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, and your browser and operating system. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

Purpose: delivering the website and keeping it secure and stable. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the reliable and secure delivery of the website. How long GitHub keeps this data is set out in its privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Transfer to the USA: GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The transfer is therefore based on the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR).

4. Contacting us and demo requests

If you write to us by email, or use the demo request on The dialogue, we receive the data you choose to give us: usually your name, email address, company and message. The demo request opens your own email program with a pre-filled message; nothing is sent until you send that email yourself.

Purpose: answering your request and, where applicable, preparing a demo or an offer. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request concerns a possible contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is answering the messages we receive.

We delete your data once your request has been dealt with and it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply (in particular six years for business correspondence under § 257 HGB and § 147 AO). Our emails are hosted by [email provider, name and address], which processes them on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

5. Fonts

The fonts used on this website (Instrument Sans, GFS Didot) are served from the same server as the website. No connection to Google Fonts or other font services is made.

6. Encryption

This website is only delivered over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS), so data between your browser and the server cannot be read by third parties.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16), erased (Art. 17) or its processing restricted (Art. 18), and to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20). To exercise these rights, write to the address in section 1.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop processing it, unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de.

8. Other information

You are not obliged to provide personal data. Without contact details, however, we cannot answer a request. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling (Art. 22 GDPR).
