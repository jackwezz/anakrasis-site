Self-hosted fonts (GDPR, see the main README, section "Fonts & GDPR"):

  InstrumentSans-Variable.woff2           Instrument Sans, variable (weight 400–700, width 75–100), latin
  InstrumentSans-Variable-LatinExt.woff2  same, latin-ext subset (loaded only for characters like "ē")
  GFSDidot-Regular.woff2                  GFS Didot, full font (includes polytonic Greek), used only for Greek words

All fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Sources: Fontsource (@fontsource-variable/instrument-sans 5.1.0) and google/fonts (ofl/gfsdidot).
The @font-face rules are at the top of assets/css/site.css.
